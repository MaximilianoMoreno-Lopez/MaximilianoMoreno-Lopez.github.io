An interactive R/Shiny prototype that visualizes how the EU Emissions Trading System extension to buildings and transport (ETS2) affects household costs across Andalusian provinces.

**Key features:**
- ERA5-Land climate data integration for temperature-driven energy demand
- Composite vulnerability index combining economic exposure and climate sensitivity
- CO₂ price scenario simulations
- Provincial-level spatial mapping

**Tech stack:** R, Shiny, ERA5-Land API, sf, leaflet
