An interactive **R/Shiny prototype** that visualises the distributional impacts of the EU Emissions Trading System (ETS2) on households across Andalusian provinces.

**Key features:**
- Composite vulnerability index combining income, energy expenditure, and climate exposure
- ERA5-Land temperature data integration for heating/cooling needs estimation  
- Adjustable CO₂ price scenarios (€40–€120/tCO₂)
- Province-level maps with interactive filtering

**Tech stack:** R, Shiny, `sf`, `terra`, ERA5-Land via Copernicus CDS API.

*In development — part of my doctoral research at PSE.*
