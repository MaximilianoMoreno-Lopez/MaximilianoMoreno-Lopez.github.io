The **Environmental Inequality Lab** is a data→dialogue→action initiative designed to engage young people in Córdoba and Almodóvar del Río in understanding and responding to heat-related environmental inequality.

Developed as part of the Anna Lindh Foundation's "Transformative Youth in Action" (MYA 2026) open call, coordinated by Estrellas del Sur.

**Methodology:** Participatory Community-Based Mapping (PCM) / Logframe approach
