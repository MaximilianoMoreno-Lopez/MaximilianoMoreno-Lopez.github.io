This paper investigates the distributional consequences of extending the EU Emissions Trading System to cover buildings and road transport (ETS2). Using household expenditure microdata combined with ERA5-Land climate data, I construct a composite vulnerability index that captures both economic exposure to carbon pricing and climate-related energy needs across Andalusian provinces.
