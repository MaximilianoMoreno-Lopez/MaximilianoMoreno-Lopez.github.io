I'm Maximiliano, a PhD candidate at the Paris School of Economics and a lecturer at Sciences Po. I study who pays for the climate transition, and how to make it fair.

My research looks at the distributional effects of climate and energy policy: how measures like the EU Emissions Trading System for buildings and transport (ETS2) reach households across incomes and regions. Most of it combines microsimulation with computable general-equilibrium models. Right now I am building a map of environmental inequality in France from micro-level data, tracking how exposure to climate shocks and energy costs varies by income, geography and household type.

I work under the supervision of Lucas Chancel, Katheline Schubert and Mouez Fodha. Before the PhD I was an economist at the Bank of Spain and at the Spanish Economic and Social Council.

Outside the PhD I run Estrellas del Sur, a non-profit I founded in Córdoba that gives young people from disadvantaged backgrounds quality education, mentoring and a route to international opportunities and scholarships.

Because where you're born shouldn't determine how far you go.
