About

I am a PhD candidate at the Paris School of Economics and Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne, funded by the Fundación Ramón Areces. My research asks who bears the costs of climate and energy policy, and how much. Most of it combines microsimulation with computable general-equilibrium models to trace how policies such as the EU Emissions Trading System for buildings and transport (ETS2) reach households across the income distribution and across European regions. I am currently building a map of environmental inequality in France from micro-level data: how exposure to climate shocks and energy costs varies by income, geography and household type.

Before the PhD I worked as an economist at the Bank of Spain and at the Spanish Economic and Social Council. Outside research I run Estrellas del Sur, a non-profit I founded in Córdoba that gives young people from disadvantaged backgrounds quality education, mentoring and a route to international opportunities and scholarships.

Because where you're born shouldn't determine how far you go.
