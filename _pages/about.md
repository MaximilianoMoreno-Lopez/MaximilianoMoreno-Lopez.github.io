I'm **Maximiliano Moreno-López**, a PhD candidate at the **Paris School of Economics** – Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne, supported by the Fundación Ramón Areces. I'm also a Junior Professor at the **Universidad de Córdoba** (Spain).

My research lies at the intersection of **environmental and energy policy** and **inequality**. I'm particularly interested in how climate change and environmental policy affect income distribution, and how economic modeling can guide fair and effective decarbonization strategies. A central focus of my work is the **EU Emissions Trading System (ETS2)** and its distributional impacts on households across European regions.

I work under the supervision of [**Lucas Chancel**](https://lucaschancel.com/en/), [**Katheline Schubert**](https://www.parisschoolofeconomics.eu/personnes/katheline-schubert/) and [**Mouez Fodha**](https://www.parisschoolofeconomics.eu/personnes/mouez-fodha/), focusing on the economics of equitable climate transitions.

I also collaborate with the **French Ministry for the Ecological Transition**, contributing to research on the macroeconomic and distributional impacts of climate policies. Previously, I have worked with the **Bank of Spain** and the **Spanish Economic and Social Council**, participating in policy-oriented economic analysis related to inequality, sustainability, and energy transitions.

Beyond academia, I am the President of [**Estrellas del Sur**](https://www.instagram.com/estrellasdelsur_ong/), a youth-led non-profit based in Córdoba (Spain) with Erasmus+ accreditation, focused on democratic participation, debate, and Model EU simulations.

In recognition of my academic record, I was awarded the **National Excellence Award** as the top economics and business graduate in Spain.
