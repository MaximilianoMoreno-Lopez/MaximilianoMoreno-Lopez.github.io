Teaching Assistant during the second semester of the academic year 2022–23, as part of my Predoctoral Fellowship at the Universidad de Alcalá.

**Course:** Microeconomics II — Markets and Imperfect Competition; market efficiency, information and market failures.

Research during this period focused on reallocation effects of minimum wage on monopsony markets in Spain and the heterogeneous effects of minimum wage on poverty and inequality (with Olga Cantó, Mariña Fernández-Salgado, and Sébastien Fontenay).
